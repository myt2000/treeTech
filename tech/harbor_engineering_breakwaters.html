Harbor Engineering (Breakwaters)港口工程（防波堤）

Artificial harbor protection using stone, rubble, timber, moles, and later concrete breakwaters to reduce wave exposure and create safer anchorages.以石料、碎石、木材、防波堤坝及后来的混凝土防波堤构筑的人工港口防护，减少波浪侵袭并营造更安全的锚地。
