Compulsory collection of goods, labor, revenues, tolls, or duties by states, temples, and local authorities, first documented through regular royal levies in the late fourth- and early third-millennium Nile Valley.国家、神庙和地方当局对货物、劳力、岁入、通行费或关税的强制征收，最早由公元前四千纪末至三千纪初尼罗河谷的王室定期征敛记录所证实。
