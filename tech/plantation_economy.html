Plantation Economy种植园经济

An economic system based on large-scale agricultural monoculture (like sugar or tobacco), reliant on an exploited labor force (often slaves or indentured servants) to produce cash crops for export.以大规模单一作物农业（如甘蔗或烟草）为基础、依靠被剥削劳动力（多为奴隶或契约仆役）生产出口经济作物的经济体系。
