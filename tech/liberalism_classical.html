Classical Liberalism古典自由主义

A liberal political tradition centered on justified and limited authority, individual liberty, rule of law, private property, and market exchange, with views ranging from minimal-state to modest public-goods provision.以正当且有限的权威、个人自由、法治、私有财产和市场交换为核心的自由主义政治传统，主张从最小国家到适度公共品供给不等。
