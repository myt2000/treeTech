Crop Rotation (Two-Field System)轮作（二圃制）

Agricultural practice of dividing land into two fields, one for crops and one left fallow to restore fertility, improving long-term yields.将土地分成两块、一块耕种一块休耕以恢复地力的农业制度，可改善长期收成。
