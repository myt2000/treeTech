Triangular Trade三角贸易

A complex transatlantic trade network connecting Europe, Africa, and the Americas. It involved shipping manufactured goods to Africa, enslaved people to the Americas (the Middle Passage), and plantation crops back to Europe.连接欧洲、非洲和美洲的复杂跨大西洋贸易网络：把制成品运往非洲、把被奴役者运往美洲（中央航路）、把种植园作物运回欧洲。
