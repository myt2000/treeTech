The Counter-Reformation反宗教改革

The period of Catholic resurgence initiated in response to the Protestant Reformation, beginning with the Council of Trent. It involved structural reforms, new religious orders like the Jesuits, and a flourishing of Baroque art.天主教会为回应新教改革而开启的复兴时期，始于特伦托会议，涉及结构改革、耶稣会等新修会及巴洛克艺术的兴盛。
