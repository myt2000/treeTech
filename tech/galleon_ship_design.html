Galleon Ship Design盖伦帆船设计

A large, multi-decked sailing ship used as both a cargo carrier and a warship. Its design integrated a lower forecastle and a square-rigged profile, making it a stable gun platform and a capable ocean-going vessel.兼作货船与军舰的大型多甲板帆船，其设计压低了前楼、呈方帆轮廓，成为稳定的火炮平台和远洋舰船。
