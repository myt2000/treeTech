Manorialism庄园制

A high-medieval Western European system of landed-estate administration in which lords, dependent tenants, customary obligations, agriculture, rents, and local jurisdiction were organized through the manor and its court.盛期中世纪西欧的领地管理制度：领主、依附佃户、习惯义务、农业、地租和地方司法围绕庄园及其法庭组织。
