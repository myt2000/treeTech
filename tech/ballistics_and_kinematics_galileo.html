Ballistics and Kinematics (Galileo)弹道与运动学（伽利略）

The mathematical study of motion, including the paths of projectiles under gravity. Galileo's work established that projectile motion could be broken down into horizontal and vertical components.对运动的数学研究，包括重力下抛体的路径。伽利略确立抛体运动可分解为水平与垂直分量。
