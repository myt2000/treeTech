Muskets (Flintlock)火枪（燧发枪）

A significant advancement in firearm technology, the flintlock mechanism used a flint striking a steel frizzen to create a spark, improving reliability, weather resistance, and rate of fire over the matchlock.枪械技术的重大进步：燧发机构以火石击打钢质击砧发火，比火绳枪更可靠、耐候且射速更高。
