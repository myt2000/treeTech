Torsion Artillery Springs扭力弹簧

Twin springs made from tightly twisted skeins of sinew or hair that powered Hellenistic bolt- and stone-throwing artillery.以紧密绞合的筋腱或发束制成的成对扭簧，为希腊化时代发射弩箭和石弹的扭力炮械提供动力。
