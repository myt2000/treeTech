Hypocaust (Underfloor Heating)火坑供暖系统（地下供暖）

A central heating system where hot air from a furnace circulates through a void under the floor and inside walls, used in villas and public baths.由熔炉热风在地板下空腔与墙体夹层内循环的集中供暖系统，用于别墅和公共浴场。
