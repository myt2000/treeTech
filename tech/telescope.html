Telescope望远镜

A refracting optical instrument that combined lenses in a tube to make distant objects appear nearer, documented in the Dutch Republic in 1608 and rapidly adapted for astronomical observation in 1609-1610.以镜筒组合透镜使远处物体看起来更近的折射光学仪器，1608年记录于荷兰共和国，1609-1610年迅速被改造用于天文观测。
