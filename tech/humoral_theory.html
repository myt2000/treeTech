Humoral Theory体液学说

A medical doctrine positing that health and illness result from the balance or imbalance of four bodily fluids (humors): blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile.主张健康与疾病源于血液、黏液、黄胆汁、黑胆汁四种体液平衡与否的医学学说。
