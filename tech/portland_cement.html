Portland Cement波特兰水泥

Hydraulic cement patented by Joseph Aspdin in 1824, made by burning and grinding limestone-and-clay mixtures into a binder for mortar and concrete.约瑟夫·阿斯普丁1824年专利的水硬性水泥，由石灰石与黏土混合物煅烧研磨而成，用作砂浆和混凝土的黏结剂。
