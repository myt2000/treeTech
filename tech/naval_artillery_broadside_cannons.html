Naval Artillery (Broadside Cannons)舰炮（舷侧齐射炮）

The practice of mounting numerous heavy cannons along the sides of purpose-built warships, allowing for devastating coordinated volleys (broadsides) that transformed naval warfare from boarding actions to artillery duels.在专门建造的战舰两舷布设大量重炮，实施毁灭性的协调齐射（舷炮齐射），把海战从接舷战变为炮战。
