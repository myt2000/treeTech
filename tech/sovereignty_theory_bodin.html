Sovereignty Theory (Bodin)主权理论（博丹）

The political concept, articulated by Jean Bodin, that the state possesses absolute and perpetual power within its territory. This theory provided a philosophical foundation for the modern, centralized nation-state.让·博丹阐发的政治概念：国家在其领土内拥有绝对且永久的力量。该理论为现代中央集权民族国家提供了哲学基础。
