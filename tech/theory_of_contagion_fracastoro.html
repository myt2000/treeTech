Theory of Contagion (Fracastoro)传染学说（弗拉卡斯托罗）

A proposition by Girolamo Fracastoro that diseases could be spread by tiny, unseeable particles ('seminaria') that transmit infection through direct contact, indirect contact, or over a distance. A forerunner to germ theory.吉罗拉莫·弗拉卡斯托罗的论断：疾病可由微小的、不可见的粒子（“传染种子”）经直接接触、间接接触或远距离传播。细菌理论的先声。
