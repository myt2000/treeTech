Mercantilism Economic Theory重商主义经济理论

Economic nationalism aiming to build a wealthy and powerful state by maximizing exports, minimizing imports, and accumulating precious metals (bullion) through state-controlled trade and colonization.经济民族主义：通过国家管控贸易与殖民，最大化出口、最小化进口并积累贵金属（金银条块）以造就富强国家。
