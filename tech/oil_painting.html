Oil Painting油画

A painting technique using pigments mixed with a drying oil (like linseed oil) as a binder. This method allowed for greater depth, luminosity, rich colors, blending, and realism than previous techniques.以干性油（如亚麻籽油）调合颜料作画的技术，比以往技法更富深度、光泽、浓彩、晕染与写实。
